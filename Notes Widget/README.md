# Notes Widget

A basic sticky-notes widget for Mac desktop. Your notes/things you need to do live right on
your wallpaper. It's pinned above the desktop, but behind all the app windows. This lets the user
glance at them or jot something down without leaving what they are
doing. Hit *Show Desktop* (or click the corner of your screen) read/edit your notes with all your tabs
and windows untouched.

## Features
- Notes that live on your desktop, behind your other windows — not floating on top
- Per-note colors with adjustable transparency
- Rich text formatting — bold, italic, underline (⌘B / ⌘I / ⌘U) and font sizing (⌘+ / ⌘−)
- Lives in the menu bar with no Dock clutter — new note with ⌘N
- Drag to reposition, resize freely, right-click to delete
- Everything saves automatically

## Built with
- Swift + AppKit (NSPanel-based desktop widgets)
- SwiftUI app lifecycle
- Native macOS — runs as a menu-bar accessory app
- Claude Code
